# AcmeCorp Internal Knowledge Base & Operations Manual

---

## 1. Human Resources & Workplace Policies

### 1.1 Remote Work & Hybrid Schedule
- **Hybrid Structure:** Full-time employees are permitted to work remotely up to **3 days per week** (typically Tuesday/Thursday in-office days, subject to team manager approval).
- **Core Working Hours:** All employees must be accessible on Slack and email during core operational hours from **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM EST**.
- **Remote Work Stipend:** Eligible remote workers receive a one-time home office setup allowance of **$500**, claimable within the first 60 days of employment.

### 1.2 Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leaves
- **Standard PTO:** Full-time staff members accrue **20 days of paid time off per year**. PTO requests longer than 3 consecutive days must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.
- **Sick Leave:** Employees receive **10 separate paid sick days annually**. A doctor’s note is required for consecutive sick leaves exceeding 3 days.
- **Parental Leave:** Primary caregivers are eligible for **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave** after 1 year of continuous service. Secondary caregivers receive **6 weeks**.

### 1.3 Code of Conduct & Anti-Harassment
AcmeCorp enforces a zero-tolerance policy for workplace harassment, discrimination, or bullying. Any violation should be reported immediately to HR via `hr-confidential@acmecorp.com` or through the anonymous compliance hotline at **1-800-555-0199**.

## 2. IT & Cybersecurity Guidelines

### 2.1 Passwords & Authentication
- **Password Standards:** Passwords must be at least **16 characters long**, include uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols, and must be changed every **90 days**.
- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** MFA is strictly mandatory across all corporate accounts using the **Okta Verify** app. SMS-based MFA is prohibited due to security vulnerabilities.

### 2.2 Device Management & Security
- **Company Hardware:** All work must be conducted on company-issued laptops. Personal devices (BYOD) are restricted to accessing web-based email and Slack only.
- **Disk Encryption:** Hard disk encryption (FileVault for macOS, BitLocker for Windows) must remain enabled at all times.
- **Software Installations:** Employees cannot install unauthorized third-party software without approval from the IT Security team. Open a ticket via the **IT Service Desk portal**.

### 2.3 IT Support Desk & Escalation
- **Portal:** Submit IT tickets at `https://service.acmecorp.internal`.
- **Emergency Hotline:** For critical production outages or lost devices, call the 24/7 IT Urgent Response desk at **extension 4357 (HELP)**.

## 3. Finance & Expense Management

### 3.1 Travel & Reimbursements
- **Submission Window:** All business travel expenses, receipts, and mileage must be submitted via **Concur within 14 calendar days** following the end of the trip.
- **Airfare Policy:** Employees must book economy class for domestic flights under 6 hours. Business class is permitted for international flights exceeding 8 hours.
- **Daily Meals Allowance (Per Diem):** The maximum allowable daily meal expenditure during business travel is **$75 per day** (Breakfast: $15, Lunch: $20, Dinner: $40). Receipts are required for any single expense over $25.

### 3.2 Corporate Credit Cards
Corporate cards are issued exclusively to department leads and frequent business travelers. Monthly statement reconciliations must be completed by the **5th of every month**. Unreconciled charges will result in card suspension.

## 4. Engineering Practices & Incident Management

### 4.1 Deployment & Code Reviews
- **Pull Request (PR) Policy:** Every code submission requires approval from at least **two senior peer reviewers** before merging into the `main` branch.
- **CI/CD Pipeline:** Automated unit test coverage must be at least **85%** for a build to pass staging verification.
- **Deployment Windows:** Production deployments are scheduled strictly between **Monday and Thursday, 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM EST**. Deployments on Fridays or weekends are strictly prohibited except for critical hotfixes.

### 4.2 Production Incident Severity Levels
- **Severity 1 (Critical):** Major service outage impacting >25% of users. **Target SLA Resolution Time: 1 hour.** On-call primary engineer paged immediately.
- **Severity 2 (High):** Degraded feature performance with a workaround. **Target SLA Resolution Time: 4 hours.**
- **Severity 3 (Low):** Minor cosmetic bug or non-essential feature failure. **Target SLA Resolution Time: 48 hours.**
